Request account or data deletion

If you have a Gambeta account and want it deleted, or want to exercise other privacy rights, you can:

  • Email support@gambetaapp.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" or "Data Privacy Request" and include the email address associated with your account.
  • Use the contact form on our website: open gambetaapp.com, go to Contact, and describe your request (including account deletion if applicable).

We process deletion and privacy requests as described in our Privacy Policy.
